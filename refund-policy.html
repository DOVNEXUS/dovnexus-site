Refund Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Our Refund Policy

DOVNEXUS wants you to be satisfied with your purchase. If you are not satisfied, you may request a full refund within 3 days of your purchase date.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact us at founder@dovnexus.com with your order number/receipt (issued by Paddle, our payment provider) and the reason for your request.

Processing

Refund requests submitted within 3 days of purchase are eligible for a full refund. Requests submitted after 3 days will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis and are not guaranteed. Approved refunds are processed by Paddle.com (our Merchant of Record) and typically appear on your original payment method within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank/card issuer.

Questions about this policy: founder@dovnexus.com
